Skimmin'

Privacy policy · last updated 24 October 2026

In short: Skimmin' does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own phone.

Who we are

Skimmin' is made by Benjamin MacDonald, an independent developer in Alberta, Canada. If you have any question about this policy, contact skimminsupport@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not include any advertising, analytics or tracking software. It does not use the internet to send information about you anywhere.

What stays on your phone

To remember your game, Skimmin' saves a few things on your own device only: your level, best scores, lake journal, daily-challenge streak, and settings such as sound, vibration and reduced motion. This information never leaves your phone and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, or by deleting the app.

Sharing your daily result

If you choose to share your daily-challenge result, the game opens your phone's normal share sheet with a short text summary. You decide whether and where to send it; nothing is shared automatically.

Apple services

Skimmin' is distributed through Apple's App Store and TestFlight. Apple may collect information such as purchases, downloads and, if you have agreed to share them, crash reports and diagnostics, under Apple's privacy policy. Any crash reports we receive through Apple are anonymous and are used only to fix problems in the game.

If a future version adds Game Center leaderboards, they are provided by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Game Center settings. We will update this page before that happens.

Children

Skimmin' is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
