Help and support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email skimminsupport@gmail.com. A screenshot and your iPhone model help a lot. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

  • Throw: drag back from anywhere on the screen, then let go. A longer pull throws harder; pulling down throws higher, across throws flatter.
  • Clean release: pull hard until the green band lights up, then let go while the needle is inside it.
  • Tap to skip: while the stone flies, tap just as it touches the water (a ring shows where it will land).
  • Replay the tips any time from Settings → How to play.

Questions

There's no sound

Check the silent switch (or Action button) on the side of your iPhone and the volume. You can also turn sound on in Settings.

Can I turn off the vibration?

Yes: Settings → Vibration.

How do I unlock more lakes?

All ten lakes are open in Free Play from the start. The Campaign visits them in order, four levels per lake.

When does the daily challenge change?

At midnight in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same lake, stone and conditions that day.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the app erases it, and it doesn't move between devices yet.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm.
